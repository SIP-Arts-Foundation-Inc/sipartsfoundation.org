Editing your page

You can update the words on your own program page any time, in about two minutes, with no code and nothing to install.

Before your first edit

  1. Create a free account at github.com if you do not have one, and send Shauna the username.
  2. Watch for an invitation email from GitHub and accept it.
That is the whole setup, and you only do it once.

Every time you want to edit

  1. Go to sipartsfoundation.org/admin
  2. Click Login with GitHub.
  3. Click your page in the list.
  4. Edit the text, then click Save.

Open the editor

Good to know

What happens after I save?

Your change goes to Shauna to approve. Once she publishes it, the live page updates on its own within a few minutes.

Can I break the website?

No. You are only editing words inside your own page, and nothing reaches the public site without approval. Edit freely.

Can I change photos, buttons, or the layout?

Not from the editor, and that is on purpose. Ask Shauna for those and they get handled for you.

Can I add an event?

Events are kept separately so the calendar and registration stay in step. Ask Shauna for access to the events list.

The login is not working.

Check that you accepted the GitHub invitation first, since the editor only opens for invited team members. If it still will not let you in, text Shauna and she will sort it out with you.
