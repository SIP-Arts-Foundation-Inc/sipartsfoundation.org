Why we need you

Southern Illinois Pathways covers nine counties. Our office is one building in Belleville. That math is the whole problem.

Families raising someone with a disability already spend their energy on appointments, paperwork and phone calls. They should not also have to drive an hour to find out what exists. Most of the support they need is already out there. They just never hear about it from anyone they know.

That is what an ambassador fixes. Not a program. A person. Somebody in their own town who already knows where the library is, which school sends the newsletter, and which group chat the parents actually read. One familiar name turns a flyer nobody opens into something a neighbor handed you.

We are not asking you to become an expert or run anything. We do the programs. You make sure your county knows they exist.

    How to earn your shirt

    Your shirt is not a signup gift. It is a thank you for bringing people with you. Three steps, all from your phone.

    1. Announce itPost your ambassador graphic and tag Southern Illinois Pathways so we can celebrate you
    2. Bring your peopleAsk your friends to like and follow our pages in that same post
    3. Come insideJoin Pathways Family Circle, our private Facebook community for families

    That is it. Your shirt comes with your county on it.
