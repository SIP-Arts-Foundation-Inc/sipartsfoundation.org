**Unmasking in the MetroEast is a community education project about autism acceptance, built around one word: masking.**

Everyone who takes part learns what masking is first, then answers it in their own work. As [AANE](https://aane.org/autism-info-faqs/library/masking-a-guide/) explains it: "Masking, also called camouflaging, is something many Autistic people do to fit in with societal expectations. It means changing, hiding, or copying behaviors to blend into neurotypical environments."

The panels are political yard signs, the ones candidates used to present the best possible version of themselves, collected before they reach the landfill and handed to artists just as they come in. Each artist decides what to do with their sign, and what you make on yours is your expression of unmasking.

Every piece is displayed at our free public showcase on April 3, 2027, held in celebration of World Autism Awareness Day.
