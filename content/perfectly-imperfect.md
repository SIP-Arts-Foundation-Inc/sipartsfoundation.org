Perfectly Imperfect Arts is a low-pressure group for people who love making things but run into real barriers getting to the table. Nobody critiques. Nobody ranks. The name is the whole idea.

## Who it's for

Neurodivergent people, and anyone facing mental health or accessibility barriers, who want a place to explore creativity and connect with others doing the same. Any experience level. If the obstacle is motivation, energy, or the weight of a blank page more often than it is skill, this group is built for you.
