Pathways Self-Advocates is a free, open group for neurodivergent youth and young adults and those with disabilities, starting at high school, across nine Metro East counties. Every month from September through April, we get together, in person or online, to learn, create, and connect with peers who get it.

No application and no experience needed. Come to one outing or come to them all.
