Pathways Self-Advocates is a nationwide advocacy, volunteer service and leadership development program of SIP Arts Foundation, Inc. Membership is free. You can join on your own, or start a club with one other person.

Every month we put an awareness topic on the air. You research it, you make it yours, you post it. That is advocacy, and it counts as volunteer service.

Core program framework

One month, one message, on repeat.

PSA runs on a monthly cycle. It is the same four steps every time, so you always know where you are in it.

Step 01 · Start of month

The prompt drops

We release awareness prompts on disability and mental health. You pick what to say about it and how to say it.

Step 02 · During the month

You make the reel

Research it, script it, record it, edit it. Working behind the camera counts every bit as much as being in front of it.

Step 03 · Post and submit

Put it on the air

Publish on Instagram, tag the account, include your PSA name and logo, then send us the link through this site.

Step 04 · Start of next month

Recognition

Two winners are announced, hours are approved for everyone whose submission was approved, and the next prompts go up.

What we ask of a submissionEvery month
  • Publish your Reel on Instagram and tag @pathwaysselfadvocates.
  • Say your registered PSA name and show your official PSA logo.
  • Follow that month's challenge and content guidelines.
  • Send the link through the submission form on this site.
  • Club leaders also list which members contributed, so each of them gets their own credit. Those names stay with us and never have to go in the public caption.
What we never askEver
  • You never have to disclose a disability or a diagnosis.
  • You never have to appear on camera.
  • You never have to have your own Instagram account. You can take part through an approved club, parent or guardian account.
  • You never lose what you have earned because you needed time away.

Recognition

Four things are counted. They are never added together.

A scoreboard is only one of them, and it is the least important. Keeping these separate is deliberate: it means a member who never wins anything still has a record that grows.

Counter 01

Membership tenure

How long you have belonged. It only ever goes up, and taking a break does not reset it.

Counter 02

Participation points

Earned for advocacy work you completed. Not for how it performed.

Counter 03

Volunteer service hours

Up to six hours for each approved monthly submission, based on the work you actually documented. Research, scripting, design, editing, accessibility review and coordination all count.

Counter 04

Competition points

Awareness reach on Instagram. Club points belong to the club; hours and participation belong to the individual members who did the work.

Volunteer verificationOn request

SIP Arts Foundation issues official service verification letters and certificates for approved hours. Whether a school, scholarship or outside organisation accepts them is up to that organisation and its own requirements, so check with them first.

Recognised every monthNot just winners
  • Two monthly winners, and you may win in consecutive months.
  • Participation milestones and membership anniversaries.
  • Volunteer service milestones.
  • Creative advocacy spotlights and community impact.
Beyond the reel

The program is bigger than the feed.

Instagram is how PSA reaches people. It is not the whole of what membership is.

Quarterly

National meetups

Optional gatherings on Zoom: new members welcomed, anniversaries marked, member spotlights, guest speakers and your say in what comes next. Cameras off is fine. Speaking is never required.

Local

Your own projects

Run an awareness event, lead an accessibility project, present to a class, facilitate a PSA meeting or team up with another club. Approved activities earn service hours too.

Ongoing

Opportunities hub

Educational, advocacy, youth leadership, conference and volunteer opportunities worth your time, gathered in one place.

Over time

Leadership pathway

Grow into an experienced advocate, an ambassador, a club leader or a peer leader. Advancement is based on what you contribute, not on who is most popular.
