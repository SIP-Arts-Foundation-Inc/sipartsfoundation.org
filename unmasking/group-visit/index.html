Unmasking in the MetroEast

Request a group visit

Bring your group to SIP Arts Foundation in Belleville. Everyone learns what masking is first, then creates on a free yard sign panel. Finished pieces are displayed at our free public showcase on April 3, 2027, in celebration of World Autism Awareness Day.

Group visits are available on Tuesdays and Saturdays. Times are flexible. There is no cost to your group.

Submitting this form is a request, not a confirmed reservation. We will contact you to finalize your visit.
