Community Partner Programs

More support in our community

These programs are hosted by trusted community partners. Registration, attendance, and any fees are managed directly by each partner. Cost should never be a barrier; email info@sipartsfoundation.org to inquire about a scholarship.

A Special Place 2 STEAM Academy

A Belleville nonprofit school built around science, technology, engineering, arts, and math, serving young scholars in Southern Illinois. Southern Illinois Pathways operates from the A Special Place campus at 424 Lebanon Ave in Belleville.

Community partner. Enrollment and programs are managed directly by ASP2.

Prairieland

Prairieland Service Coordination

An Independent Service Coordination agency serving individuals with developmental disabilities across Illinois, including Madison and St. Clair Counties. PSCI handles PUNS registration, eligibility determination, and linkage to waiver services.

Services provided and managed directly by PSCI. Maryville office: 2130 Vadalabene Dr, Ste A, Maryville, IL 62062 · 618-288-1897.

Visit Prairieland Service Coordination

Open Arms Social Support Group

Virtual support groups for those on the spectrum or with special needs (ages 9 and up), plus a separate group for parents, caregivers, and special educators. Led by a licensed special education teacher.

1st Monday monthly: Children, teens & young adults
3rd Monday monthly: Parents & caregivers

Hosted by Open Arms. Registration is managed directly by Open Arms.

We Rock the Spectrum — Edwardsville

An inclusive kids' gym designed for children of all abilities, offering sensory-safe equipment, open play, and family-friendly events in Edwardsville.

Hosted by We Rock the Spectrum. Visit their page for hours and programs.
